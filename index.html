Google Scholar Bookmarklet

Citation Distribution & h-index Visualizer

A self-contained, zero-dependency browser tool to parse any Google Scholar profile, sort individual paper citations, and chart the citation distribution curve with exact h-index threshold marking.

Drag the blue button below to your browser's Bookmarks Toolbar. Then navigate to any Google Scholar profile and click it to visualize the citing distribution!

⚡ How to Install

  1. Ensure your browser's Bookmarks bar is visible (Press Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the blue "📊 Scholar Distribution" button up into your Bookmarks bar.
  3. Or: Click "Copy Code", create a new bookmark manually, and paste the code into the URL field.

🎯 How to Use on Google Scholar

  1. Open any profile, e.g. Angelo Salatino's profile.
  2. Click the Scholar Distribution bookmarklet in your toolbar.
  3. A floating card will automatically expand all papers on the profile, sort them in descending order, plot the distribution curve, and mark the h-index cutoff line!

📐 What the Chart Shows

  • X-axis: Papers sorted descending by citations (toggleable to ascending).
  • Y-axis: Number of citations (toggle between Linear and Log10 scale).
  • Red Vertical Line: Exact boundary where papers have citations ≥ h. Papers to the left form the h-core.
  • Yellow Dashed Line: Horizontal reference at citation count = h.
  • Auto-Load All Papers: Automatically clicks "Show more" on launch so the full publication history is analyzed (with a Stop button if you want to pause early).
